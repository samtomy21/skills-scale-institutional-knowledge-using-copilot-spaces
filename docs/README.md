## OctoAcme Project Management at a Glance

### Our Principles

- **Customer-first**: prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: encourage feedback and learning

### Key Roles and Responsibilities

OctoAcme operates with three primary personas that drive our project delivery:

- **Project Managers (PMs)**: Coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. Enable teams to deliver on commitments efficiently.
- **Product Managers (PdMs)**: Define what should be built, own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes to drive customer value.
- **Developers**: Design, build, test, and deliver software components while collaborating on design, quality, and acceptance criteria.
- **QA/Testing**: Validate quality and acceptance criteria throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a structured five-phase approach:

1. **Initiation** - Define the problem statement, identify stakeholders, establish high-level timeline, and secure approval to move forward
2. **Planning** - Break work into shippable increments, establish acceptance criteria, identify dependencies, and create a release plan
3. **Execution** - Build, test, review, and iterate on features with continuous feedback and tracking
4. **Release** - Deploy to production, verify functionality, and announce the release to stakeholders
5. **Close & Retrospective** - Capture learnings, document improvements, and convert them into actionable next steps

### Communication Cadence

Consistent communication ensures alignment and transparency across all stakeholders:

- **Weekly PM + Product Manager sync** - Strategic alignment and decision-making
- **Twice-weekly delivery team standups** - Progress tracking and blocker resolution
- **Monthly stakeholder updates** - Status reports and milestone achievements
- **Ad-hoc escalations** - Immediate communication for critical issues or decisions

### Key Artifacts

Projects maintain several key documents and artifacts:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** - Problem statement, goals, success metrics, and timeline
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** - High-level milestones and delivery schedule
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** - Prioritized work items with acceptance criteria
- **Definition of Done** - Clear quality and completion standards for all deliverables
- **Risk Register** - Tracked risks with impact, likelihood, and mitigation plans
- **Retrospective notes** - Learnings and action items from completed projects or sprints

## OctoAcme Project Management Overview

OctoAcme operates a structured yet iterative project management framework built on five core principles: customer-first thinking, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. The organizational model is centered around Project Managers who coordinate delivery and risk management, Product Managers who define outcomes and prioritize work, and Developers who implement features while collaborating on design and quality.

Execution at OctoAcme emphasizes transparency, collaboration, and continuous feedback through a defined communication cadence and workflow structure. Teams operate on a predictable rhythm: daily standups to surface blockers and progress, weekly syncs between PM and Product Manager, twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, and monthly stakeholder updates. Work is managed through GitHub Projects using a standardized workflow, with pull requests kept small and requiring approval before merging. Dependencies and risks are actively managed through a Risk Register reviewed weekly during syncs, with a three-level escalation path ensuring blockers are quickly resolved.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout OctoAcme's delivery process rather than confined to a single phase. Unit tests, integration tests, and end-to-end smoke tests validate functionality before release, with automated security scanning in the CI pipeline and manual QA for feature acceptance when needed. Release management is standardized with pre-release checklists ensuring all acceptance criteria are met, CI passes, and rollback plans are documented. Post-deployment verification and stakeholder announcements complete the release process.

The organization reinforces continuous improvement through structured retrospectives held after each sprint, release, or significant milestone. These sessions capture what went well, what could improve, and generate prioritized action items with clear owners and due dates. This combination of defined workflows, proactive risk management, embedded quality practices, and a culture of learning ensures OctoAcme consistently delivers reliable, valuable solutions while building institutional knowledge across the organization.
